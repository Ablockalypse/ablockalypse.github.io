Arena Sweep

Prove the tag.
Clear the court.

Laser Tag Demo is a free Solo Arena Sweep sample — first-person, clinical, score-attack. Each court runs the same loop: brief, two waves, clear. Nine targets. Score and accuracy count.

Mirrored high-tech dark hall. Cyan emissive beams. Reflective floor. Single-player only — no networked play, no co-op.

The demo

What you get

  • Free Solo Arena Sweep training lane — short sessions, finishable
  • Skippable intro, then three courts (A → B → C)
  • Nine targets across two waves per court; score and accuracy on clear
  • Digiflux / Vulkan ray-traced lighting (RTX-capable recommended)
  • Windows and Linux builds on itch.io

What this is not

  • Not multiplayer — no local co-op, no networked laser tag
  • Not the full Laser Tag product
  • Not a lore dump — mode-forward training lane once you are in-court
  • Not the Ra Engine SDK or a shared fiction world

Requirements

Windows and Linux. The ray-traced presentation is meant for RTX-capable GPUs. Expect reduced quality or performance on hardware without that support.
