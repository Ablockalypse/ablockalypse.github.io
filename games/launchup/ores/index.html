This table is design SoT. The playable slice today is the Lattice climb.

Progression

Four pillars

  1. Dig — break surface/crust for raw ore
  2. Refine — smelt / crush / synthesize into materials
  3. Fabricate — materials → parts on benches / printers
  4. Assemble — parts → escape vehicle → larger ships

Automation comes online after the first escape chassis flies (conveyors, drills, smelters). Every ore has a clear next use within one or two tiers — no dead rocks. Tier 5 proper names are gated and stay TBD.
