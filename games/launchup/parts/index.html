03

Minimum Viable Escape

Required to leave the pocket / crater. All nine parts fabricated and assembled on a pad → first launch mission.

MVE kit — nine part types (roadmap)
Part Materials (sketch) Notes
Escape chassis (pod frame) Steel + aluminum Physics body; seats 1
Pressure hull plates Steel / aluminum Integrity for vacuum
Cockpit canopy Glass (silicon) Visibility
RCS thruster ×4 Steel + copper coils Attitude
Main thruster (chemical) Steel + propellant chem Boost out of gravity well
Propellant tank (S) Aluminum Paired with main thruster
Battery pack (S) Copper + carbon Bus power
Flight computer Copper + silicon Controllable craft
Landing legs Steel Soft touchdown

Physics sandbox: mass, thrust, and fuel matter — aluminum vs steel is a real trade.
