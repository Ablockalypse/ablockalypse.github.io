Active BBS directory

A worldwide directory of systems currently published in the live public BBS lists fetched for this page — not every unpublished private board. Filter by language or search across English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Russian, and more. Primary credit: Telnet BBS Guide, with companion files from that guide and the Synchronet public list. Also folded in the 8-Bit Boyz directory, the Chinese-language Telnet BBS List, the Dock Sud Latinoamerica list, the FLY BBS Russian telnet list, the Commodore 64 BBS List, The BBS List, Japanese grass-roots lists, Brazil’s RBT national list, and other current public directories. Names, hosts, ports, sysops, locations, and languages come from those pages and careful location cues — they are not invented here. Telnet boards open in the in-browser client at /telnet/. The light on each card is a connection check from this directory, not a login test.
